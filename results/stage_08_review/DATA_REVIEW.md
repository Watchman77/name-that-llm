# V20 data, training provenance and evaluation review

Reviewed the captured public V20 source and author description for `kirilltym/0-600-teach-the-model-remove-the-prompt`, script version **360088024**, on 20 October 2026. This was static inspection only. No downloaded code was executed, no checkpoint or dataset bytes were opened by this reviewer, and no fitting, provider calls, browser interaction or publication occurred. Parent-collected page observations establish the displayed author score; this review does not independently reproduce that score.

## What changes relative to our admitted experiment

Our Stage 07B admission covered **750 first-party OpenAI V4 `new` responses**, 150 five-label prompt blocks, for an undistributed private classifier. It did not approve every public dataset, every provider route, generative-model training, or distribution of trained weights. The two-stage LoRA described here is a classification adaptation with plain/centred classification heads, not evidence of a generative fine-tune objective; nevertheless, it is a new data and checkpoint scope.

V20 uses at least three distinct external-data surfaces:

1. The main n-gram classifier reads a first-found `pool_v1.parquet` and fits vocabulary, IDF and its SVC on official train plus the pool. The inference source does not rebuild the pool, assert its published 34,940 count, validate requested/served identities, compare its rows against official responses, or verify a pinned digest. Its class weighting is balanced over the combined fit, rather than our matched-objective-mass 07B intervention.
2. Final `lora8b_v1` and `lora14b_v1` adapters and their `heads.safetensors` are described as trained first on that 34,940-row pool, then for two epochs on all 2,699 official rows. The inference file validates the heads' label order from `meta.json`, but does not independently establish the training corpus hashes, checkpoints' actual training history, fold exclusion, or claimed source-training recipe.
3. The embedded complementary 15% stack has a separate `load_extra_passages()` loader for `ntl_extra_passages_claude.csv`, `gen_passages.csv`, `ntl_generations.csv`, `ntl_proxy_generations.csv`, `ntl_generated*.csv`, and `passages_clean.csv`. The captured preview reports **7,644 rows**, not 34,940. It filters declared completed outputs for some schemas, normalizes whitespace, retains 30–300 words, deduplicates model/text and prompt/model, and requires at least 12 labels per prompt. Only `passages_clean.csv` IDs receive a distinct bank prefix. It does not establish cross-source prompt-bank identity, remove official-response overlaps, or authenticate provider identities. The embedded code also fits family gate objects although the final composite omits applying those hard gates; unused prediction gates do not mean their fit operations never occur.

The author lists fourteen pool datasets: seyitkaangunes, willrice, lxdlxdlxd, prvsiyan extended and own-prompt, alperen5252525, haileycheng, kaplinskiy, lucasboesen free-proxy, abdullah1afify, dophuchung, ziyuzhou1207, keshavatearth, and michaelsemenoff. These source names are declarations, not a verified row-level manifest. The description declares CC BY 4.0 for most and CC BY-SA 4.0 for prvsiyan's own-prompt source. Its stated punctuation, markdown, length, exact-duplicate and official-response filters require verification against the actual pool production recipe and bytes.

## Which validation claims can be verified here?

**The author's prompt-grouped CV figures are not independently reproducible from this inference notebook alone.** The narrative reports 0.569 for each two-stage LoRA and 0.593 for the smaller ensemble, but no actual five-fold training loop, immutable split manifest, fold-specific stage-2 adapters, OOF predictions or fold results are supplied by the captured file. The file runs final models on `test.csv`; it does not run those CV calculations. The embedded smoke option makes pseudo features and is a code-path test, not a measured reproduction of the real classifiers.

The final adapters have reportedly seen every official row during stage 2. Holding out prompts only when fitting a new readout or Gaussian head on these final adapters would **not** produce honest prompt-excluded development predictions: representation training has already used those held-out labels. Fold-specific training exclusions must extend through the supervised adapter stage. This is a constraint on a proposed local validation design, not a finding of hidden-test label leakage in the author's legitimate final-train/test inference.

The main new Gaussian is fitted on final-8B features of official training rows. That is normal for a final inference fit, but its in-sample pair probability check only checks shape/finiteness/normalization. Likewise, the embedded logs explicitly label several checks as seen-in-training. They are implementation checks, not development accuracy.

The captured author preview processes just **three rows**, all singleton clusters. The new grouped Gaussian contribution is fitted but has no eligible public-test group. The final full Sinkhorn is skipped for fewer than 50 rows. Successful preview execution therefore does not independently exercise actual grouped-test behaviour, full-test memory/runtime, or the hidden-test rerun. The page's author public score is a separate platform observation, not our preview result or our score.

## Minimum evidence before an execution decision

- Pin V20 and every attached dataset, model and notebook-output version; capture the exact source digest and checkpoint/pool digests. Confirm first-found glob paths resolve to those intended bytes, with one unambiguous `pool_v1.parquet` and each required adapter/head marker.
- Inspect pool production manifest/schema/counts/class supports, source-version and route/requested/served-ID fields where available, official-response deduplication, and documented prompt grouping. Check the separate 7,644-row branch too; the 34,940-row pool audit does not cover it automatically.
- Obtain checkpoint `meta.json`/configuration, base model revisions, classification-head class order, training data digests and training completion evidence. Record which identity or collection facts remain author assertions. Do not label a public checkpoint as trained only on our admitted subset.
- For an unchanged public-inference reproduction, preserve source behaviour and record it as such; do not claim original-method value or new prompt-grouped CV. Full-test runtime and an actual score in our own account are separate milestones from the author's three-row preview and displayed 0.600.
- For an independently validated improvement, obtain genuine held-out fold checkpoints and OOF provenance, or retrain the chosen classifier through prompt-excluded supervised stages under a separately frozen protocol. Repartitioning the already inspected 150 prompts is not fresh independent evidence. Do not use final all-official adapters as a shortcut to that claim.

**Decision from this review:** the method is materially different and worth dependency/provenance inspection, but data admission and training/CV reproduction are not established by the visible inference source. This is not a blanket rejection, blanket permission, or a request to buy API access. No actual reproduction result exists for our account.
