# Stage 14B: Paired prompt-contrastive development experiment

**Question.** Does a small projection trained to recognize the same model across different prompts correct errors missed by the existing frozen-feature control? This uses the same 150 official training prompts reused in development, with a different, externally declared partition. It measures development balanced accuracy, not a Kaggle public score or the performance of full S07.

**Inputs.** Competition 170484; raw GLM feature cache from our Stage 06C V1 359405443 (layers 8 and 3); frozen DeBERTa features from Lucas Boesen 358745836 (layers 24 and 6); and the five split declarations from the explicitly pinned artifact hub V22. Stage 14A bound the split file digest `bf073280a69cf3d427b14e2c057dd5c7b5199051cdbdc8e3c49fcf0926cd4bad` and prompt-fold digest `449f2facc165d51d1898d1225eba8a30fa4a280d126722376a6704e6950d4b36`. Each split has 120 fitting prompts and 30 held-out prompts; every official row is scored once. No native cache logits, native adapter weights, external labeled rows, test rows, or prior GLM out-of-fold probabilities are read. The notebook is private, offline, CPU only.

**Arms.** Refit the established scaled GLM and DeBERTa logistic heads, using the existing 75:25 probability mixture. Train two otherwise identical 64-dimensional L2-normalized projections on the concatenated features. The first uses cross-entropy (CE); the second adds 0.20 times a contrastive term inspired by [supervised contrastive learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11362), at temperature 0.1, with same-label/different-prompt positives and different-label/same-prompt hard negatives. Preprocessing is fit on outer training rows only. Each pair uses the same initialization, a fixed schedule of eight whole prompts per batch, 20 epochs, AdamW with learning rate 0.001 and weight decay 0.01, and seed 141400 plus zero-based fold. Evaluate control and the two fixed geometric mixtures (80% control, 20% new head).

**Decision.** Primary is row-independent balanced accuracy. Require at least +1.0 percentage point versus control and positive improvement versus the CE mixture, with both paired 95% bootstrap lower bounds above zero, and no class losing more than 5 points against either reference. Resample whole prompts within fixed folds 4,000 times with seed 141401, holding predictions fixed. Also report rescues, regressions and difficult unassigned corrections. Secondary applies the unchanged Stage 13F text grouping, within-fold uniform Sinkhorn, and 12–18-row assignment equally to all arms. Secondary is diagnostic; a batch of related passages can couple errors, so its fixed-prediction interval is conditional. A primary pass motivates validation with fresh evidence and does not authorize official submission.
