## Original modelling prerequisite

There is currently **no honest full-S05 out-of-fold prediction set**. Final adapted checkpoints saw the official training labels; Stage12 also mixes fold adapters with full-fit adapters/readouts. A simulated validation split applied after those fits does not remove that leakage.

Before claiming a confusion matrix or development improvement for the complete ensemble:

- Inventory every supervised adapter, readout, Gaussian head, preprocessing fit and calibration/blend fit.
- Require verifiable prompt-fold training/exclusion provenance for every prediction. Where that provenance is unavailable or the component was fit on held-out labels, refit from a suitable untouched checkpoint within the outer prompt fold or explicitly exclude it from a labelled proxy.
- Keep all same-prompt responses together and fit learned preprocessing/calibration/blending within the training side. Canonical existing folds have been reused and remain exploratory, not a fresh holdout.
- Estimate free-resource costs before any full refit. No claim that a faithful large-ensemble OOF run is ready or affordable follows from this plan.
